Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 30, 2026  ·  Last Updated: May 3, 2026

The short version: MemTool collects only what it needs to work. Your email is for login only — not for marketing, tracking, or advertising. We don't sell your data. You can delete your account and everything associated with it at any time.

1. Who We Are

MemTool is operated by OC2CO ("we," "us," or "our"). We build tools that help you organize your thoughts, train your memory, and track your wellness. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect when you use MemTool and why.

If you have questions, contact us at support@oc2co.com.

2. Data We Collect

We collect only what's necessary to provide the app's functionality:

Authentication

Email address & password

Used exclusively to create and secure your account. Your email is never used for marketing, advertising, or third-party sharing.

Your Content

Memories & thoughts

Text you capture in the app. Stored so you can access, search, and review your own content. Only you can read it.

Your Content

Mood logs

Daily mood and wellness entries you choose to log. Stored for your personal tracking and trend review.

Performance

Game scores & stats

Your brain-training game performance data, stored so you can track your own progress over time.

AI Features

Daily recap data

AI-generated summaries created from your own memories and mood entries. Cached to reduce API calls and improve performance.

Voice & Audio

Voice captures (where supported)

If you use voice input to create memories, audio is processed to generate a text transcript. Raw audio is not permanently stored. Transcription may be handled by a third-party service (see Section 4).

Biometrics (optional)

Health & biometric sync

If you grant permission to sync health data (e.g., from Apple Health), that data is stored and used exclusively to personalize your wellness insights. We do not share biometric data with third parties. You can revoke this permission at any time in your device settings.

Billing

Subscription status

Whether your account is free or Pro tier. Payment processing is handled entirely by Stripe, Apple, or RevenueCat — we never store your card details.

Analytics

Anonymous usage events

Basic aggregate events (app_open, feature used) to understand how the app is used at a population level. These are anonymous and not tied to your identity or content.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • Provide the service — authenticate you, store your memories, generate recaps, track game progress.
  • Process payments — manage your subscription tier via Stripe and Apple IAP.
  • Improve the app — aggregate, anonymous analytics to understand which features are used.

We do not use your data for advertising, marketing, behavioral profiling, or sale to third parties.

AI Features & Your Data

MemTool includes AI-powered features that process your personal content to generate responses and insights. This section explains what data each AI feature uses and what it does not do.

AI Feature

Daily Recap

Your memory captures and mood entries from the day are sent to OpenAI to generate a personalized daily summary. This summary is cached and shown in the app. Raw content is sent to OpenAI as described in Section 4.

AI Feature

AI Insight

Your mood logs, game performance data, and memory patterns are used to generate a short personalized wellness insight. This data is processed by OpenAI to identify patterns and trends in your activity.

AI Feature

AI Guide (Mem)

When you chat with Mem, your AI Guide conversation history, recent memories, 7-day mood trends, and game statistics are sent to OpenAI to generate a personalized response. This context is used solely to make Mem's replies relevant to your situation — it is not stored by OpenAI beyond their standard API data handling policy.

AI Feature

Daily Coaching Prompt

One AI-generated coaching prompt is cached per user per day based on your activity patterns. It is regenerated daily and uses the same context as the AI Guide.

Important Limits

AI Guide (Mem) is NOT a healthcare provider

Mem is a pattern-reflection tool — not a therapist, not a counselor, and not a crisis service. Mem does not provide medical advice, mental health diagnosis, or treatment recommendations. Nothing Mem says should be interpreted as professional mental health or medical guidance. If you are experiencing a mental health crisis, contact the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline by calling or texting 988, or go to your nearest emergency room.

4. Third-Party Services

MemTool integrates with the following third-party services. Each has its own privacy policy:

  • OpenAI — powers AI daily recaps. Your memory content is sent to OpenAI to generate summaries. OpenAI's data handling is governed by their privacy policy.
  • Stripe — handles payment processing for Pro subscriptions. We never see or store your payment card details. Stripe's privacy policy.
  • Apple In-App Purchase — handles iOS subscription billing. Governed by Apple's privacy policy.
  • RevenueCat — subscription management and synchronization across platforms. RevenueCat's privacy policy.
  • Neon (PostgreSQL) — database hosting. Your data is stored in encrypted PostgreSQL databases on Neon's infrastructure.
  • Cloudflare R2 — object storage for user-generated files (e.g., attachments or exported data). Files are stored in encrypted buckets. Cloudflare's privacy policy.
  • Render — cloud hosting provider that runs the MemTool backend server. Your data transits through Render's infrastructure. Render's privacy policy.

5. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored in encrypted PostgreSQL databases hosted on Neon. Data is transmitted over HTTPS. We use industry-standard security practices to protect your information.

No method of electronic storage is 100% secure. If you become aware of a security concern, contact us immediately at support@oc2co.com.

6. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, all associated data (memories, mood logs, game stats, recaps) is permanently deleted within 30 days.

Anonymized aggregate analytics data (not tied to any individual) may be retained indefinitely for product improvement purposes.

7. Your Rights

  • Access — you can view all your data within the app at any time.
  • Export — contact us to request an export of your data.
  • Correction — update your memories, mood logs, and account info directly in the app.
  • Deletion — delete your account and all associated data via the app settings, or by emailing us.
  • Portability — request a copy of your data in a standard format.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at support@oc2co.com.

8. California Residents (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):

  • Right to Know — the categories of personal information we collect and how we use it (see Sections 2 and 3 above).
  • Right to Delete — request deletion of your personal information (see Section 7).
  • Right to Opt-Out of Sale — we do not sell personal information. There is nothing to opt out of.
  • Right to Non-Discrimination — we will not discriminate against you for exercising your privacy rights.

9. Wellness Data — Not Medical Advice

MemTool's mood logs, wellness tracking, brain-game performance data, and AI-generated insights are for personal reflection and self-improvement only. Nothing in MemTool — including AI-generated summaries, wellness patterns, or psychological insights — constitutes medical advice, mental health treatment, diagnosis, or professional counseling.

Do not use MemTool as a substitute for professional medical or psychological care. If you have concerns about your mental or physical health, consult a qualified healthcare provider.

10. European Residents (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, or Switzerland, you have rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or equivalent laws:

  • Right of Access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification — request correction of inaccurate personal data.
  • Right to Erasure — request deletion of your personal data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Right to Restrict Processing — request that we limit how we use your data in certain circumstances.
  • Right to Data Portability — request your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Right to Object — object to processing of your personal data for certain purposes.

Our legal basis for processing your data is contract performance (to provide the service you requested) and legitimate interests (service improvement). To exercise any GDPR rights, contact us at support@oc2co.com. We will respond within 30 days.

11. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

MemTool is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, contact us immediately and we will delete it promptly.

Users between 13 and 17 should have parental consent before using MemTool.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. Material changes will be communicated in-app. Continued use of MemTool after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

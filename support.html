Frequently Asked Questions

How do I upgrade to Pro?

Open the app, go to Settings, and tap the Upgrade to Pro card. You'll be taken to a secure checkout page ($4.99/month).

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions can be cancelled through the platform where you purchased: the App Store (iOS Settings → Subscriptions), Stripe (via the customer portal link in Settings), or RevenueCat-managed stores. Email us at support@oc2co.com and we'll assist.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings in the app and tap Delete Account. This permanently deletes all your memories, mood logs, game stats, and AI Guide history. Subscription records are retained for legal and billing compliance. This action cannot be undone.

Why is the AI Guide (Mem) not responding?

Mem uses OpenAI to generate responses. During high-demand periods the service may be temporarily unavailable. Try again in a few minutes. If the issue persists, email us.

Is my data private?

Yes. Your memories and mood logs are private to you. We never sell your data. AI features use OpenAI's API to process your content — see our Privacy Policy for full details.

I found a bug. How do I report it?

Email support@oc2co.com with a description of the issue, your device, and steps to reproduce. We'll look into it promptly.
